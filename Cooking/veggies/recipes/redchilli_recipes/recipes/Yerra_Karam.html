Yerra Karam Recipe

×
❮ ❯

Description

A Hot and Spicy chutney served for breakfast. It's great for Idlies and Dosa.

Ingredients

  • 1 Onion, 3 Tomatoes,30 kashmiri chillies, 1 whole garlic.
  • 2 tbsp oil for Saute
  • 15 tbsp oil for Tempering
  • 1tsp jeera/cumin seeds
  • 1tsp mustard seeds

Instructions

  1. Cut ,Cube and Peel the onion, tomato, chilli and garlic.
  2. Add oil and Saute onion pieces along with chilli pieces for 2 mins.
  3. Add garlic pieces too and saute for 2 more mins.
  4. Add tomato pieces and saute for 5 mins and let it cool for 30mins.
  5. Pour all the the sauted mixture into mixer and make a smooth paste.
  6. Karam Paste is ready for Tempering/Tadka/Tiragamatha.
  7. Put 15 TBSP of oil, add jeera and mustard to the oil and let it splutter.And add the karam paste.
  8. Let it stay on stove for some mins until the rawness is gone.
  9. Stir once in a while.
  10. Final ready-to-serve Yerra Karam is ready.
  11. The Ingredients specified makes Karam for 30 dosas. You can refrigerate for 2 weeks.
