Vankaya Masala Curry

❮ ❯

Description

A simple and most delicious Eggplant recipe perfect for lunch or dinner. It's great for rotis or rice.

Ingredients

  • Total 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1 long Eggplant or 250 gms Brinjal
  • 1 onion chopped
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 1 big tomato
  • 1/2 tsp chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp corriander powder
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 cup tamarind pulp
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Chopped Corriander leaves

  • For paste :

    • 2 tbsp coconut pieces
    • 2 tsp sesame seeds
    • 8 garlic cloves
    • 1/2 tsp cumin
    • 1/2 tsp pepper

Instructions

  1. Heat a pan and pour 1tbsp of oil on it.
  2. Cut the eggplant into cubes and add to the pan with a pinch of salt . Saute for 2 minutes and keep it aside
  3. Make the paste with the list specified in the ingredients.
  4. Add 1tbsp oil and saute chopped onion with curryleaves and saute for few mins.
  5. Add chopped tomato and saute for a min.Add chili,corriander and turmeric powders and saute for a min
  6. Add the grounded paste and saute for 3 mins.
  7. Add Fried brinjal.Add Tamarind pulp and water and mix well. Cook it with lid on for 6 to 8 mins.
  8. Garnish with corriander leaves.
  9. Serve hot with rotis or rice.
