Gandhi and Three Monkeys

2 Min read

The story of Gandhi's three monkeys is about a gift of a Chinese toy that taught Gandhi the principle of "see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil". Gandhi kept the toy with him for the rest of his life.

What do the monkeys represent?
Mizaru: Covers his eyes and sees no evil
Kikazaru: Covers his ears and hears no evil
Iwazaru: Covers his mouth and speaks no evil

**What's the significance of the story?
The toy taught Gandhi the principle of "see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil"
The phrase "see no evil, hear no evil, speak no evil" became a message of peace and tolerance
The toy is a visual metaphor that encourages people to avoid negative actions
