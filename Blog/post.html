About Goddess GayathriDevi

Jan 24 2025 4 Min read

Gayatri (Sanskrit: गायत्री, IAST: Gāyatrī) is the personified form of the Gayatri Mantra, a popular hymn from Vedic texts. Gayatri mantra has 24 letters. That is why it called as gāyatrī caturviṃśatyakṣarā (Sanskrit: गायत्री चतुर्विंशत्यक्षरा). They are 1.tat, 2.sa, 3.vi, 4.tur, 5.va, 6.re, 7.ṇi, 8.yaṃ, 9.bhar, 10,go, 11.de, 12.va, 13.sya, 14.dhī, 15.ma, 16.hi, 17.dhi, 18.yo, 19.yo, 20.naḥ, 21.pra, 22.cho 23.da and 24.yāt. The 24 Letters of Gayatri mantra represent 24 Tattvas.[20] They are
1.Five Bhūtas, namely, pṛthivi (Earth), apas (Water), agni (Fire), vāyu (Air) and ākāśa (Sky).
2.Five Tanmātras, namely, gandha (smell), rasa (taste), rūpa (form), sparśa (touch) and śabda (sound).
3.Five Karmendriyas (i.e. motor organs), namely, upasthā (sexual organ), pāyu (anus), pāda (leg), pāni (hand) and vāk (mouth).
4.Five Jñānendriyas (i.e. sense organs), namely, ghrāna (nose), jihvā (tongue), caksus (eye), tvak (skin) and śrotra (ear).
5.Four Vāyus (air), namely, Prāṇa, Apāna, Vyāna and Samāna

The well known form of Gayatri with the Saivite influence appears having five heads (Mukta, Vidruma, Hema, Neela, Dhavala) with the ten eyes looking in eight directions plus the earth and sky, and ten arms holding various types of weapons attributed to Shiva, Vishnu, and Brahma She is shown holding lotus, noose, trident, Scimitar and vard mudra in right whereas conch, discus, bow-arrow, goad and abhaya mudra in left.

Om Bhur Bhuva Swaha,
Tat Savitur Varenyam,
Bhargo Devasya Dhimahi,
Dhiyo Yonah Prachodayat.

Om – it is considered the primeval sacred sound from which all sounds emerge. Also, Aum is Brahma and a metaphor for Source Energy or the Supreme Being. It is usually said that Om is the greatest of all mantras.
Bhur – it is the embodiment of vital spiritual energy (prana).
Bhuva – it represents the astral plane of existence (which is of the nature of subtle elements)
. Swaha – it represents the embodiment of happiness.
Tat – it represents Brahman – The Ultimate Reality, which is simply referred to as “That” because it defies description through language or speech.
Savitur – it represents the source of all creation, supreme reality, divine illumination (of Goddess Shakti or Savitri).
Varenyam – it represents the Supreme Consciousness that inspires adoration.
Bhargo – it represents the destroyer of sins, radiant one, one who illumines all.
Devasya – it represents the divine, of the deity.
Dhimahi – it represents the meditation or knowledge of the Absolute.
Dhiyo – it is the intellect, awareness, intelligence, reasoning, and discriminating faculty which is an instrument for attaining higher wisdom.
Yonah – it represents our energy, our actions.
Prachodayat – it translates as may inspire, stimulate, propel towards the higher reality.
